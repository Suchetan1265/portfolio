Quant · Python2025

algo-trader

Cost-aware backtesting framework

A lightweight, transparent backtesting engine for US-market strategies in Python/pandas. Strategies emit a target weight per bar; the engine executes on the next bar to avoid look-ahead bias, charges a realistic turnover-based cost model (slippage + half-spread + commission), and compounds a net equity curve against a buy-and-hold benchmark. Ships Z-score (Bollinger) and RSI(2) mean-reversion strategies, Yahoo-Finance data with a synthetic fallback, and a roadmap to Alpaca paper trading.
